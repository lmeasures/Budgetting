# Requirements

## Problem Statement

Currently, spreadsheet(s) are used to perform budgetting, requiring constant reworks, fixes and updates. Not to mention the difficulty in navigating the UI, the slow updates, and the lack of any-and-all integration capability.


## Concept

A streamlined and straightforward budgetting app to eliminate the need for complex and painful spreadsheet(s). Allowing for a better end-to-end budgetting experience, with minimal requirement for user(s) to provide formulas, and overall reduce the time required to *do* budgetting.
